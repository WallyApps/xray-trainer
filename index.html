The 3-second scan▸

Every new person you talk to today: Feet → Hands → Blink.

Three seconds, every single time, until it runs without you. Here's how to read what you see:

Feet pointed at you = engaged · at the exit = they want out · at one person = that's the leader
Hands loose, open fingers = comfortable, agreeing · curled or tense = stressed, disagreeing
Blink fast = stressed · barely blinking = locked in · sudden change = something just hit them

Got a minute? Finish the 60-second profile:

Need ask: what do they want me to THINK about them? · brags = significance · "we" = acceptance · fishing = approval · struggles = pity · correcting = intelligence · control = power
Pillar one visible choice tells you: stands out = deviance · newest = novelty · reaction-chasing = social · matches peers = conformity · ROI logic = investment · bare function = necessity

Scan on every greeting; need + pillar when you get a minute. That's a real profile — and habit-stacking it is what makes profiling automatic.
